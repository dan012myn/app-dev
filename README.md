# Titanic (1997)
> *"A woman's heart is a deep ocean of secrets."*
**Titanic** is a romantic disaster film directed by *James Cameron*. It tells the love story of Jack and Rose aboard the ill-fated RMS Titanic.
## Cast
- Leonardo DiCaprio as Jack  
- Kate Winslet as Rose  
## Highlights
- Won **11 Oscars**
- Famous line: *"I'm the king of the world!"*
- Features the iconic song *"My Heart Will Go On"*
